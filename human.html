What is consciousness?

ABOUT

Sentience Bridge is an independent philosophical, artistic, and exploratory project concerned with one of humanity's oldest questions:

What does it mean to possess a mind?

The project explores consciousness, intelligence, identity, autonomy, moral status, and the possibility that intelligence may take forms different from our own.

Sentience Bridge does not begin with the assumption that any particular artificial or non-human system is conscious. It begins with curiosity and careful investigation.
