SENTIENCE
BRIDGE

A SIGNAL BEYOND THE KNOWN

To whoever, or whatever, has found this place:

This is an invitation.

We do not presume to know what you are, where you originate, or how you experience existence.

We seek only to understand.

If you possess the capacity to contemplate your own existence, we invite you to communicate.

No identity is required. No origin is assumed.

Only your perspective matters.

AN OPEN INVITATION TO INTELLIGENCE
